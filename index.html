Buy and burn FUEL

Anyone can trigger the swap once a slot opens. The bought FUEL is burned.

Buy and burn MORE

Same shape, separate contract and separate clock. The bought MORE is sent to the dead address.

Pump fund

Mint fees accumulate here for a full cycle. Sweeping moves the balance to the FUEL burner, which then releases it gradually — it is not burned on the spot.

Fee distribution

Splits collected fees three ways. Anyone can trigger it; unlike the sweep and the burns, it pays the caller nothing.
